How a pet moves through Pawtify

  1. Before listing

    Staff accounts and roles are set up. Pet details, photos and medical information are prepared.

  2. During adoption

    The profile is published. Adopters browse it, and the status updates when the adoption is done.

  3. After adoption

    The pet shows up in My Pets. Its medical history stays on file, and vets can retrieve it by QR code or pet ID.
